title=Climb Ladders
description=Adds the right-click options [b]Climb up the ladder[/b] and [b]Climb down the ladder[/b] on any ladder in the game, including ones you place yourself.
description=
description=[h2]The problem it solves[/h2]
description=In Build 42, several ladders in the base game have the [i]ladderN[/i] or [i]ladderW[/i] property but not the [i]climbSheetN[/i] / [i]climbSheetW[/i] flag, which is what the engine checks to let you climb. The result is that the same ladder can be climbed in one orientation but not the other.
description=
description=This affects the metal ladder (industry_railroad_05_37), as well as sewer ladders, wooden ladders, and billboard ladders.
description=
description=[h2]How to use it[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]To [b]climb up[/b]: stand next to the ladder and right-click on it, or simply press [b]E[/b].
description=[*]To [b]climb down[/b]: stand at the top, within one tile of the opening, and right-click on the ground. You don't need to see the ladder.
description=[/list]
description=
description=If there's no floor directly above the ladder, it looks for the adjacent tile the ladder leads to. When there's nowhere to exit, the option appears grayed out explaining why.
description=
description=[h2]Details[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Does not modify the saved world or any game files.
description=[*]Recognizes ladders by sprite name, so it works with all 14 base-game ladders.
description=[*]Uses a bit of endurance per section, like climbing a rope.
description=[*]Client-side Lua only.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Requires Build 42.
